ShareShelf only collects what it needs to match supplies between students. This page explains what that is.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, school email, program and a securely hashed password.
  • Posts: the items you share and the requests you make, including notes, dates and pickup spots.
  • Handoffs: meetup times and places you agree on, and whether an item was handed over or returned.

We never ask for your home address, phone number or payment details.

Who sees it

  • Students at your school see your name, program and the items you share.
  • Your request notes, meetup details and loan dates are only shown to the student on the other side of that request.
  • Your email is used for your login and password reset. It isn’t shown to other students.

How we use it

To run ShareShelf: show listings, suggest matches for your requests, remind you about handoffs and returns, and keep the community safe. We don’t sell your information or use it for ads.

Your choices

  • Edit your name and program any time in Settings.
  • Pause or remove items, and cancel requests, from your dashboard.
  • To delete your account and its data, contact the ShareShelf team.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and tell you in the app.
