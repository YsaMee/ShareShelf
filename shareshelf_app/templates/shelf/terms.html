ShareShelf helps students at the same school give away, lend and find academic supplies. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

1. Who can join

You need a school email address ending in .edu.ph. One account per student. Keep your password to yourself; you’re responsible for what happens on your account.

2. What you can share

  • School supplies, books, tools and materials you own and are free to give or lend.
  • No selling. ShareShelf is for free giving and lending only, with no payments.
  • Nothing illegal, unsafe, stolen, or against school rules.
  • Describe the item and its condition honestly.

3. Requests and handoffs

  • Only request items you actually need, and cancel requests you no longer need.
  • Meet at the on-campus pickup spot you agreed on. Never share or ask for a home address.
  • Show up at the time you accepted, or let the other student know as early as you can.

4. Lending

  • When you borrow, return the item by the date you agreed to, in the condition you got it.
  • Loans can’t go past the lender’s limit or the end of the school term.
  • If something gets lost or damaged, tell the lender right away and work it out together in good faith.

5. Respect

Be kind and honest with classmates. Harassment, spam and misuse of other students’ information aren’t allowed. Accounts that break these rules may be paused or removed.

6. No guarantees

Items are shared between students “as is.” ShareShelf connects you but doesn’t inspect items or take part in handoffs, so check an item before you accept it.

7. Changes

We may update these terms. If the change matters, we’ll let you know in the app before it takes effect.
